## 1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURAL PHILOSOPHY

HP Edit Enterprise has been engineered not as a conventional corporate brochure, but as an **outcome-driven digital operating system**. Every customer touchpoint focuses on customer outcomes (*"You"*, not corporate *"We"*), eliminates IT buzzword fluff, presents factual engineering data, and offers interactive utility tools that convert executives, founders, and SMEs into qualified inbound pipeline.

Underneath the public layer lies an **air-gapped, bot-cloaked, RBAC-hardened Enterprise Admin Portal** with immutable security auditing, granular feature toggle switches, and full-stack CRM capabilities.

## 7. ENTERPRISE ADMIN PORTAL (`/admin` & `/admin/dashboard`)

### A. Stealth Cloaking & Anti-Bot Defense System
- **Stealth Cloaking Headers**: Automatically injected on all admin routes via Next.js middleware:
  - `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet, notranslate, noimageindex`
  - `Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0`
  - `Pragma: no-cache`
- **Honeypot Trap Fields**: Invisible inputs that detect and immediately block automated crawler scripts.
- **IP Rate Limiting & Threat Logger**: Automatically logs suspicious login attempts, brute-force scans, and unauthorized access attempts into the Security Threats feed.
- **Custom Stealth Slug Support**: Admin URL route can be renamed from settings.

### B. Dead-Hard RBAC & Multi-Role Governance
- **Role Hierarchy**:
  - `SUPER_ADMIN`: Complete CRUD control over all system assets, team members, credentials, feature toggles, and settings.
  - `SALES_LEAD`: Access to Inquiries CRM, Kanban Pipeline, Video Bookings, and Client Projects.
  - `CONTENT_MANAGER`: Access to Services Bento, Case Studies, Tech Blogs, Team Bios, and FAQs.
  - `TALENT_HR`: Access to Careers Inbox and Job Applications ATS.
  - `SECURITY_AUDITOR`: Read-only access to Audit Logs, Threat Radars, and Compliance Trails.
- **Session Authentication**: Secure HTTP-only cookies with cryptographic tokens.
- **Team Credential Management**: Super Admin can add team members, reset passwords, change roles, and update credentials.
- **Self-Service Access Request System**: Unauthenticated or restricted team members can submit access elevation requests for Super Admin review.
